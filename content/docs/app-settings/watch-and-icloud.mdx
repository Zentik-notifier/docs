## iCloud

Synchronize your notifications and buckets with iCloud to keep them in sync across your Apple devices. Sync is required to have notifications available on Apple Watch.

| Option | Default | Description |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| **Sync active** | Off | Enable iCloud synchronization via CloudKit |
| **Notification Limit** | Unlimited | Maximum number of notifications to sync |
| **Debug logs** | Off | Enable verbose CloudKit logging (useful for troubleshooting) |

### Actions

- **ReSync to CloudKit** — Force a full re-sync of all data to iCloud
- **Delete data** — Remove all synced data from iCloud

## Watch

Apple Watch related settings, including watch authentication.

### Apple Watch Token

Manage the token used by the Apple Watch app to perform notification actions and send the server address to the Watch.

- **Update Watch data** — Push the latest token and server address to the Watch
- **Regenerate Token** — Generate a new token for the Watch app
- **Delete Token** — Remove the Watch token

After updating, open the Watch app to receive the new settings.
