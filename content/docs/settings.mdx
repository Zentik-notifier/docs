# Settings

Configure the app to your preferences. Settings are available from the app (web and mobile).

---

## Appearance

Control how the app looks: light theme, dark theme, or automatic based on your device.

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Light** | Always use the light theme. |
| **Dark** | Always use the dark theme. |
| **System** | **Automatic**: the app follows your device’s light/dark setting. If the system is in light mode, the app uses the light theme; if the system is in dark mode, the app uses the dark theme. This is the default so the app stays consistent with the rest of your OS. |

You can change the theme from the header (theme switcher) or from the user menu. Your choice is saved and applied across sessions.
